About This Project

Hi, I'm Kiya.

I'm a creative studio owner and digital designer, and the mind behind Cryptid Studio. I build intuitive tools and celestial-themed experiences that help people see something about themselves — or the world — a little more clearly.

This astrocartography tool is part of that same world. It maps the exact spots on Earth where each of your planets was rising, setting, or at its highest point the moment you were born — and calculates the whole thing live in your browser, no server involved. I built it because most astrocartography tools I'd used felt like reading a spreadsheet: accurate, but cold. I wanted something that actually explained what a line meant and why it mattered, not just where it was.

Whether you're plotting your next move, curious about a place you've never been, or just want to know which corner of Melbourne your Jupiter line runs through — this was made for you.
